Here's what I read in 2025 - in order of what I liked most (highest rated first):

### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12839928-yugandhar' target='_blank'>1. युगंधर [Yugandhar]</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/01.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Sawant, Shivaji
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
कृष्ण के जीवन का अत्यंत सुंदर और संवेदनशील चित्रण। विशेष रूप से अर्जुन, रुक्मिणी, उद्धव, द्रौपदी, सात्यकि और राधा जैसे निकट जनों की दृष्टि से उनके व्यक्तित्व के विविध पक्षों को समझना एक अद्भुत अनुभव है। उनका जीवन-दर्शन अतुलनीय है और महाभारत के युद्ध को कृष्ण के दृष्टिकोण से देखना अत्यंत प्रभावशाली लगता है।<br/><br/>पुस्तक के दो भाग विशेष रूप से गहन और स्मरणीय हैं। पहला, गीता का उपदेश और दूसरा, महाभारत के बाद द्वारका लौटकर सुधर्मा सभा में अपने पतियों और बेटों को खो चुके लाखों परिवारजनों को यह समझाना कि यह युद्ध क्यों आवश्यक था और इतनी व्यापक क्षति के बाद मानवता ने इससे क्या पाया।
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/211399783-the-thinking-machine' target='_blank'>2. The Thinking Machine: Jensen Huang, Nvidia, and the World's Most Coveted Microchip</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/02.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Witt, Stephen
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
A pacy and exciting read filled with unconventional wisdom and unique perspectives from Jensen Huang. Not a hagiography. Reminded me of Shoe Dog in thrill.<br/><br/><i>”Technical debt is the battle scar of survivors.”</i>
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25666050-algorithms-to-live-by' target='_blank'>3. Algorithms to Live By: The Computer Science of Human Decisions</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/03.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Christian, Brian
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
Brilliantly shows how computer science algorithms can guide everyday decisions. Concepts are insightful and explained simply. It deepens the belief that programming computers teaches you how to think.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/218699854-the-art-of-batting' target='_blank'>4. The Art of Batting: The Craft of Cricket’s Greatest Run Scorers – WILLIAM HILL SPORTS BOOK OF THE YEAR NOMINEE 2025</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/04.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Kimber, Jarrod
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
A delightful ode to the art of batting. Packed with engrossing anecdotes &amp; rich cricketing lore. Sharp analysis across eras, bowling attacks, and batting styles. You may not agree with every ranking, but the stories will keep you hooked. <br/>A gem for every cricket lover. Specially if you have loved reading Jarrod’s essays.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/48640699-bahut-door-kitna-door-hota-hai' target='_blank'>5. Bahut Door, Kitna Door Hota Hai</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/05.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Kaul, Manav
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
बहुत ही निजी और खूबसूरत यात्रा वृतांत। यूरोप के छोटे शहरों को आप मानव के साथ महसूस कर रहे होते हैं। कुछ वाक्य और विचार बहुत मज़ेदार लगे। <br/><br/>“यहाँ हरा इतना ज़्यादा हरा था कि लगता था आँखों ने मानो पहली बार यह रंग देखा हो।” <br/><br/>“इन्हें बेंच रखना आता है। आप जहाँ सोच सकते हो कि यहाँ बैठने में कितना मज़ा आएगा, वे वहाँ बेंच रख चुके होते हैं।”<br/><br/>“इस शहर की छोटी-पतली गलियों में घूमने लगा, मानो इस शहर की नसों में से होकर गुज़रना चाहता हूँ।”<br/><br/>“सुबह आज बहुत सुबह ही हो गई।”<br/><br/>“अंत की रेखा पर टहलना और उसे पार न करना बड़ी गुदगुदी-सी देता है।”
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/59575939-an-immense-world' target='_blank'>6. An Immense World: How Animal Senses Reveal the Hidden Realms Around Us</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/06.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Yong, Ed
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
“An Immense World” truly lives up to its name - opening your eyes to the vast, unseen ways animals perceive the world. It gives you a new lens to appreciate your own senses and the richness around you. It enriches your perspective. And your Umwelt.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/39800154-deewar-mein-ek-khirkee-rahati-thee-3rd' target='_blank'>7. Deewar Mein Ek Khirkee Rahati Thee (3rd)(3rd) (Hindi Edition)</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/07.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Shukla, Vinod Kumar
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
विनोद कुमार शुक्ल जी को शायद यह पता नहीं था की उन्हें “दीवार में एक खिड़की रहती थी” लिखनी है। पर इस किताब को ये बात पता थी। इसलिए यह ख़ुद को वहीं लिखवाती रही जहाँ विनोद जी कुछ लिख रहे होंगे। <br/>खिड़की के उस पार की दुनिया इतनी मासूम और मनोरम है कि पढ़ते पढ़ते मन करता है, काश अपने घर की दीवार में भी ऐसी एक खिड़की होती। प्रेमचंद सी सरलता और एक ख़ूबसूरत सी बेफिक्री है इस कहानी में।
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/68428.Mistborn' target='_blank'>8. Mistborn: The Final Empire(Mistborn, #1)</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/08.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Sanderson, Brandon
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
Brilliant and unputdownable!<br/>Mistborn is a whirlwind adventure with heart-pounding action and banter that’s pure gold. The only downside? It ends. But as Sanderson so perfectly puts it:<br/><br/><i>’Going?' Vin asked. 'But you just got in!' 'That’s the funny thing about arriving,' he winked. 'Once you’re there, the only thing left is to leave.'</i>
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/8785617-mansarovar---vol-2-short-stories-by-premchand' target='_blank'>9. Mansarovar - Vol. 2; Short Stories by Premchand</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/09.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Premchand, Munshi
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
बेहतरीन कहानियाँ। कानूनी कुमार, दो बैलों की कथा, मिस पद्मा, लॉटरी और शुद्रा खासा पसंद आयीं।
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/57423646-immune' target='_blank'>10. Immune: a Journey into the Mysterious System that Keeps You Alive</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/10.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Dettmer, Philipp
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
A very simple yet powerful explanation of the human immune system. The book makes you marvel at how our body constantly fights off enemies. It honestly feels like a miracle that we don’t die every minute. An excellent introduction to the immune system and its fascinating intricacies.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/171681821-the-anxious-generation' target='_blank'>11. The Anxious Generation: How the Great Rewiring of Childhood Caused an Epidemic of Mental Illness</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/11.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Haidt, Jonathan
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
“The Anxious Generation” delivers a deeply researched, unsettling look at the damage addictive social media inflicts on children and teens. Like cigarettes in the ‘70s, its harm is underplayed today—will we regret it in 20 years? Only time will tell.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/30658.Meditations_on_First_Philosophy' target='_blank'>12. Meditations on First Philosophy</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/12.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Descartes, René
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
Reads a bit bland. Arguments being repeatedly derived from God’s existence feel tedious.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/317910.Hind_Swaraj_or_Indian_Home_Rule' target='_blank'>13. Hind Swaraj or Indian Home Rule</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/13.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Gandhi, Mahatma
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
Hind Swaraj is a worthwhile read to understand Mahatma Gandhi’s ideas on self-rule and local governance. Some ideas may feel absurd, but they need to be viewed in the context of a book written over 100 years ago. What stands out is the sharp clarity in Gandhi’s thinking, even when one disagrees with his conclusions.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55928578-the-hero-with-a-thousand-faces' target='_blank'>14. The Hero with a Thousand Faces</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/14.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Campbell, Joseph
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
“The Hero with a Thousand Faces” is an eye-opener in how global mythologies echo the same core themes. Loved the framing of mythology as collective psychology—not to be judged by historical accuracy, but by what societies choose to believe. Myths reflect the values people want to live by, and the heroes they aspire to become.<br/><br/>The constant hopping between myths, though rich, could’ve been more coherent.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/2784.Ways_of_Seeing' target='_blank'>15. Ways of Seeing</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/15.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Berger, John
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
“Ways of Seeing” offers a compelling lens on how we view art, especially paintings and images, tracing their evolution through history. While art enthusiasts may appreciate it more deeply, I found the final chapter—drawing parallels between classical oil paintings and modern advertising—particularly enriching.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/22033680' target='_blank'>16. ठुमरी</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/16.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: रेणु, फणीश्वर नाथ
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
“पंचलाइट”, “तीर्थोदक” और “तीसरी क़सम” — तीनों ही कहानियाँ बेहद उम्दा हैं। हर कहानी में ग्रामीण बिहार की मिट्टी की सोंधी ख़ुशबू और वहाँ की सरल, सहज ज़िंदगी की झलक मिलती है।
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3828902-thinking-in-systems' target='_blank'>17. Thinking In Systems: A Primer</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/17.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Meadows, Donella H.
<br>
My rating: ★★★☆☆
<br><br>
"Thinking In Systems" offers valuable frameworks and perspectives for understanding and shaping teams, systems, and projects. The flow of ideas is a bit disjointed which hinders engagement. It could have been more compelling with a better structure.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/19463257' target='_blank'>18. अकबर</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/18.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: सांकृत्यायन, राहुल
<br>
My rating: ★★☆☆☆
<br><br>
“अकबर” पूरी नहीं पढ़ पाया। राहुल सांकृत्यायन की ‘वोल्गा से गंगा’ बेहद पसंद आई थी, लेकिन यह किताब शायद मेरे लिए नहीं थी।
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/54550359-work' target='_blank'>19. Work:  A History of How we spend our Time</a>
<img src="/assets/images/2025_books/19.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: Suzman, James
<br>
My rating: ★★☆☆☆
<br><br>
"Work" struggles with clarity and purpose, making it feel stretched and unfocused. While it offers a few interesting facts, it lacks the depth and density of insights you'd expect from a book in this genre. Ultimately, it fails to hold your attention.
<br clear="all"><br>
