Here's what I read in 2023 - in order of what I liked most (highest rated first):

### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/40718726-genghis-khan-and-the-making-of-the-modern-world' target='_blank'>1. Genghis Khan and the Making of the Modern World</a>
<img src="https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1530716694i/40718726.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/2497.Jack_Weatherford' target='_blank'>Jack Weatherford</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
This book feels a bit biased towards the Mongols but if you’re looking for an introduction to the Mongol empire and Genghis Khan, this gives a perfect breezy start. The secular ideals, introduction to paper currency, facilitating exchange of ideas & science between Europe and Asia, and their influence on Renaissance and the modern culture is fascinating to read.

A cool trivia for computer scientists - the origin of the word “algorithm” traces its roots to the Mongol empire.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/60321447-chip-war' target='_blank'>2. Chip War: The Fight for the World's Most Critical Technology</a>
<img src="https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1662566820i/60321447.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/10070.Chris_Miller' target='_blank'>Chris Miller</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
A riveting page turner!
Chip War offers a captivating and comprehensive history of the semiconductor industry, deftly intertwining technological innovations with geopolitical maneuvers. Beginning in the Cold War era, the book charts the journey through decades of innovation and rivalry, culminating in the recent intensification of US-China tensions over chip dominance.

<img src="https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1348344833i/177068.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>

### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/139470389-vajpayee' target='_blank'>7. VAJPAYEE: The Ascent of the Hindu Right, 1924–1977</a>
<img src="https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1682868202i/139470389.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/25228129.Abhishek_Choudhary' target='_blank'>Abhishek Choudhary</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
A good, factual account of Vajpayee’s life and the rise of India’s right wing. It has fascinating details on how India’s first formidable opposition came together. The dilemma in Vajpayee’s belief systems and his/ his party’s reluctance to speak it out aloud is brought out well.

His transformation into a person who could bring different factions together during the 70s is interesting. The book ends with removal of Emergency - looking forward to the next part.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/75669073-coaching-beyond' target='_blank'>8. Coaching Beyond: My Days with the Indian Cricket Team</a>
<img src="https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1673252432i/75669073.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/7320313.R_Sridhar' target='_blank'>R. Sridhar, R. Kaushik</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
This book gives a good peak into decision making in the Indian cricket team over the last 10 years. Enjoyed reading about thought process behind things like Ashwin’s exclusion in overseas tests, no.4 position during 2019 world cup, the Australian summer during Covid 19, etc. Some of the anecdotes give great insights on how top athletes think.

The contrast between leadership styles of Dhoni, Virat, Rohit & Ajinkya was brought out well. It’s one of the better books on Indian cricket.
<br clear="all"><br>
