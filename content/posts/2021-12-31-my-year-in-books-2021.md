Here's what I read in 2021 - in order of what I liked most (highest rated first):


### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/22609391-the-wright-brothers' target='_blank'>1. The Wright Brothers</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1430942575l/22609391.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/6281688.David_McCullough' target='_blank'>David McCullough</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
The Wright Brothers is an exhilarating account of inventing flying machines. It gives deep insights into the Wright family and their relationships. It brings out the value system that the two brothers grew up with and maintained throughout their life. The spirit of endeavor, immaculate study & experimentation is inspiring. <br/> David McCullough's writing style is endearing. The book deserves an extra star for stunning photographs of the first flyers of humankind.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/6900.Tuesdays_with_Morrie' target='_blank'>2. Tuesdays with Morrie</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1423763749l/6900.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/2331.Mitch_Albom' target='_blank'>Mitch Albom</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
Morrie has a very stoic take on life, death & everything in between. I didn't know it's a memoir before completing it. <br/> <br/> Reading Morrie talk about death without any self-pity was illuminating. Mitch Albom succeeds in not letting the book become preachy.
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/15881.Harry_Potter_and_the_Chamber_of_Secrets' target='_blank'>3. Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets (Harry Potter, #2)</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1474169725l/15881.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/1077326.J_K_Rowling' target='_blank'>J.K. Rowling</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★★
<br><br>
Apart from a couple of irritating characters, I was hooked throughout. I found it more engrossing than the first one given that the world was already built. I had a magical Sunday with this book. <br/> <br/> <i>"It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities."</i>
<br clear="all"><br>

### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/12007777-why-socialism' target='_blank'>6. Why Socialism?</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1637581438l/12007777.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/9810.Albert_Einstein' target='_blank'>Albert Einstein</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
Einstein's paper is condensed and touches upon an individual's relationship with society. Paragraphs like the following show great foresight: <br/> <br/> <i>"...the members of legislative bodies are selected by political parties, largely financed or otherwise influenced by private capitalists who, for all practical purposes, separate the electorate from the legislature. The consequence is that the representatives of the people do not in fact sufficiently protect the interests of the underprivileged sections of the population. Moreover, under existing conditions, private capitalists inevitably control, directly or indirectly, the main sources of information (press, radio, education). It is thus extremely difficult, and indeed in most cases quite impossible, for the individual citizen to come to objective conclusions and to make intelligent use of his political rights."</i>
<br clear="all"><br>



### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/41881472-the-psychology-of-money' target='_blank'>7. The Psychology of Money</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1581527774l/41881472.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/7499284.Morgan_Housel' target='_blank'>Morgan Housel</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
Sound, easy-to-comprehend financial advice. This book's "wisdom per page" is through the roof.  <br/> <br/> I especially liked the last two chapters where the author elaborates on his own financial journey & how the changing economy has broadened the economic divide leading to growing populism.
<br clear="all"><br>

<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1432531998l/25596931.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>

<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
तालाबों के इतिहास और पानी के संरक्षण पर लिखी कोई किताब इतनी ख़ूबसूरत हो सकती है, ये मेरी कल्पना से परे था। ये किताब भारत के तालाबों, उन्हें बनाने वालों, और बनाने की रीतियों में छुपे विज्ञान से आपका परिचय कराती है। <br/> ये किताब उन तालाबों की याद दिलायेगी जो आपके बचपन के इर्द-गिर्द थे, पर अब गायब हो चुके हैं।

### <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//book/show/154126.The_Discovery_of_India' target='_blank'>9. The Discovery of India</a>
<img src="https://i.gr-assets.com/images/S/compressed.photo.goodreads.com/books/1358746649l/154126.jpg" alt="cover image" height='300' width='200px' style='float:left; padding-right:20px; padding-bottom:5px; padding-top:5px'>
Author: <a href='https://www.goodreads.com//author/show/89095.Jawaharlal_Nehru' target='_blank'>Jawaharlal Nehru</a>
<br>
My rating: ★★★★☆
<br><br>
Nehru is a romantic and India is his muse. "Discovery of India" has sublime prose & eloquence. The book feels like being on a historical tour of India with a guide who is hopelessly in love with her. <br/> <br/> More than the historical tidbits, I liked his interpretation of them. His statesmanship shines through these interpretations. <br/> <br/> The chapters on last 100 years could have been shorter but that's understandable as that timeline coincides with his own life. Inside accounts of the freedom movement are interesting. <br/> <br/> His writing shows that politics' gain was literature's loss.
<br clear="all"><br>
